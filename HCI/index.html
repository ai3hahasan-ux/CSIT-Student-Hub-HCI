Chairman's Message

❝

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science & Information Technology at NED University of Engineering & Technology. Our department has been at the forefront of producing highly skilled computing professionals who contribute meaningfully to industry, research, and society.

We are committed to providing a rigorous, innovative, and student-centered academic environment. Through our BS, MS, and PhD programs, we equip our graduates with both technical excellence and the analytical mindset required to lead in an increasingly technology-driven world. I encourage every student to engage actively, explore research opportunities, and make the most of your time at CSIT.

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Mubashir Khan
Chairman, Department of CSIT — NEDUET
